📊 Your Popularity Source

This one choice decides which popularity value powers all your scores - opportunity, keyword insights, and download estimates, on every tab. Click a card to select it. Switching is instant and fully reversible.

{% csrf_token %}

Whichever you choose, both values stay visible side by side everywhere popularity appears - only the math changes. Selecting recalculates every score immediately.

What exactly changes when I switch?
  • Changes instantly, including your full history and trends: popularity, opportunity, insight labels, and download estimates - everywhere in the app.
  • Never changes: difficulty. It measures your competition, not search volume.
  • Keywords outside Apple's top terms stay consistent: under the Apple source, keywords not among Apple's top 500 per category are scored with the calibrated estimate, aligned to Apple's 1-100 scale and marked EST* - one consistent ruler across all your keywords.
  • Saved AI analyses keep their numbers: each report states which source it used, so it always matches the AI's written strategy. New and refined runs use your current selection.
  • AI aggregate scores follow the source: scores computed from popularity (for example the Simulator's ASO Readiness) can differ noticeably between sources - the result page tells you when the other source would paint a different picture.
  • Nothing is lost: both values keep being recorded, so switching back restores exactly what you had.

Which should I choose?

We recommend Apple Ads: where Apple publishes data, it is Apple's own measurement, and where it does not, that absence is itself real information about search volume. The RespectASO estimate remains a full-coverage, open-methodology alternative - and it always stays visible beside Apple's numbers.
