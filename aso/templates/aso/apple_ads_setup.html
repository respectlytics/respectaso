Connect Your Apple Ads Account

A one-time, five-minute setup that unlocks Apple's own keyword popularity as a source in RespectASO. Free - no campaigns, no spend, and no payment method required.

1 Get an Apple Ads account (skip if you have one)

Go to ads.apple.com and sign up with your Apple ID. Choose the Advanced plan when asked - it's the free self-service tier, and creating the account costs nothing.

You do NOT need to create a campaign, add a payment method, or spend anything. The account only gives RespectASO permission to read Apple's keyword-popularity data on your behalf.

2 Sign in from RespectASO

Open Settings → Popularity Source and click "Sign in with your Apple Ads account". A secure Apple sign-in window opens - enter your Apple ID, password, and the two-factor code, exactly as in a browser.

The window closes by itself once you're in. Your session is stored only on this Mac, in your RespectASO data folder, with owner-only file permissions. RespectASO never sees or stores your password.

3 Set your Primary App ID

Enter the numeric App Store ID of any app your Apple Ads account can access - usually your own app. If you track your app in RespectASO, the settings page offers it as a one-click fill. You can also find the ID in any App Store link: it's the number after id in apps.apple.com/app/id1234567890.

This ID is only request context required by Apple's interface - it does not limit which keywords you can look up.

4 Test, sync, and choose your source

Click "Test connection" - RespectASO fetches one sample keyword to confirm everything works. Then the Apple Ads card becomes selectable: choose it as your popularity source, and run "Sync now" to pull Apple values for all your tracked keywords.

From then on everything is automatic: keywords you add are fetched from Apple the moment they are scored, values refresh once a day, and all syncing is politely paced with automatic backoff if Apple slows us down.

What to expect

  • • Apple values are granular (any number 5–100) and shown with an ASA badge; the RespectASO estimate stays visible right beside them.
  • • Many keywords come back at the scale's minimum (5). Apple's data does not differentiate between keywords reported at 5 - they may differ in real search interest, but the data cannot show it. The estimate beside them keeps differentiating in that range.
  • • Very small keywords have no Apple value - Apple simply doesn't report below its volume threshold. Those keywords automatically use the estimate, marked EST*.
  • • Apple sign-ins expire after a while. When that happens you'll see a banner; one click signs you back in. Your scores keep working the whole time (the estimate covers the gap).

Troubleshooting

"Primary App ID is not accessible for this account"

The app id you entered doesn't belong to (or isn't visible to) your Apple Ads account. Use your own app's id, or any app listed in your Apple Ads console.

"Apple rejected the session - sign in again"

Your Apple session expired (this is normal every few weeks). Click "Sign in with your Apple Ads account" again.

Sync says "Rate limited by Apple"

Nothing to do - RespectASO already slowed down and will finish the remaining keywords automatically on the next sync cycle.

Most keywords show EST* instead of ASA

Run "Sync now" first; if they persist, those keywords are below Apple's reporting threshold - the estimate is the only available value for them, which is exactly what the fallback is for.

The AI Simulator readiness score looks much lower with Apple Ads

When many analyzed keywords sit at Apple's scale minimum (5), opportunity - and the readiness score built on it - is capped by that limited resolution. The result page shows an advisory with the exact counts and what the same metadata would rate under the RespectASO estimate. A low score there reflects Apple's numbers, not necessarily misaligned metadata.

What happens if I sign out?

Your source selection is kept - nothing changes behind your back. Scores keep using your last-synced Apple values; new keywords use the estimate. While Apple Ads is your active source, a banner reminds you until you sign in again or switch source. Signing back in re-runs the connection test automatically.

Using the browser/Docker edition?

The embedded sign-in window needs the RespectASO desktop app. Sign in from the desktop app once - the session lives in your data folder and syncing works from there.
